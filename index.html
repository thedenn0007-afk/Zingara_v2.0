Our Story

Not all biryanis are bold in colour.

Some speak in subtler cues — layered, delicate, and deeply rooted in tradition. Zingara is a study in restraint.

Born from Mysore's culinary legacy, our white biryani is a quiet declaration. No artificial colour, no shortcuts. Only the craftsmanship passed down through generations.
