From Our Kitchen

What Makes White Biryani Different?

White biryani is an act of restraint. It's not the absence of flavour — it's the discipline to let flavour speak without colour crutches. The cream hue comes from milk, ghee, and jeera rice. The flavour comes from 19 spices ground fresh every morning.

The 19-Spice Blend: A Mysore Tradition

Our 19-spice blend is not a recipe. It's a ritual. Sourced from Mysore's spice markets, ground by hand in our kitchen each morning. The proportions have been refined over years. Together they form the backbone of everything we cook.

Why We Don't Add Colour

The biryani world is obsessed with colour — saffron yellows, turmeric oranges, food-dye reds. We think that's a distraction. Colour in food is a signal, and we want our signal to be flavour. When you see white biryani, you know exactly what you're getting.

The Art of Dum Cooking

Dum is a Persian cooking technique where food is sealed in a pot and slow-cooked over low heat. At Zingara, we dum cook every biryani in a traditional handi. No shortcuts. No pressure cookers. Every batch, every day.
